About this app

Every safety app a child can defeat, a child will defeat

Blockers get switched off. Schedules get learned. Trackers get resented, and the relationship pays for it. The failure is always the same: the product makes the child its adversary.

JoanX takes the other side. While your child is walking, it watches for one thing — walking and phone use at the same time — and answers with a buzz, then a sentence, then their own buddy asking them to look up. Stopping pays: points, a buddy that visibly grows, villains that can finally be beaten. Nothing is ever taken away.

It runs on the phone your child already has. No wearable, no GPS trail — just the accelerometer, and only while they are walking.

The safety moment · F-07 → F-12

Ten seconds, one buzz, and a sentence that firms up

This is the whole product; everything else exists to make a child care how it ends. The timings are server-tunable — these are the values the prototype ships.

0 – 10 s · grace

Nothing happens

Walking plus phone use has to hold for ten seconds. A child already putting the phone down is never warned.

10 s · buzz

One vibration

A single buzz. Never repeated — no screen, no attention lock, no embarrassment in front of friends.

+2 s · hold

Still on the phone?

Stop inside two seconds and no warning is ever drawn. The most common path through this feature has no UI at all.

warning

A card, then the buddy

One line and one button, pinned low so the path ahead stays visible. Five seconds later the buddy speaks.

+5 s · cooldown

Silence, then firmer

Dismissing buys five quiet seconds. If the risk is still there, the next round is firmer — up to three.

1 · Gentle 2 · Firm 3 · Urgent It gets firmer. It never gets mean — and the buddy's face is never lighter than the words.

The game

One currency, and it is only ever earned by walking

No gems, no energy, no second currency to put a paywall behind. Every price on the shop shelf converts directly into an amount of safe walking.

Buddies

Levels 1 to 10 across three stages — Hatchling, Growing, Guardian. The transformation fires wherever the level-up happens, even mid-battle.

  • Evolution grants no stats — it is art, never power.
  • Maxing one buddy is about 19 hours of safe walking.
  • Then you hatch another and grow a different one.

Eggs

Common eggs cost 500 points, Rare 1,500. Epic eggs cannot be bought at any price.

  • A 30-day streak hands over an Epic egg outright.
  • A 7-day streak, 100 safe km, or halving phone use each unlock a buddy.
  • A child who never spends a point can still reach the rarest buddies.

For guardians

Evidence the behaviour is changing, not a feed to watch

The lead number is never an incident count. It is how much less often this is happening than in your child's own first week — because ranking children punishes whoever started worst, and fear only sells once.

One week, four numbers

Warnings that ended in a verified stop, safe-walking minutes, seconds to look up, and the streak — each against last week.

A summary that opens with the verdict

Plain language and one hero number first, the narrative second, and exactly one thing to try this week.

An impact takes the whole screen

A hard knock asks the child if they are okay for twenty seconds. No answer, and it reaches you with the last known place and time.

Groups, not one household

Up to five circles — parents, the aunt who does Tuesdays, the school-run crew. You join by scanning a QR, and an admin has to accept. Each child has one main guardian per group.

Data safety

The child is shown this list too

Word for word, on their own profile screen, with the guardians named. Anything you can see, they know you can see — which is the only version of this that survives a ten-year-old finding out.

Shared with the guardian

  • How long they walked safely minutes
  • Warnings while walking and how each ended
  • Points and streak the game layer
  • Which app types are blocked categories only

Never leaves the phone

  • Where they are no GPS in scope
  • Their messages and guestbook never read
  • Their photos never read

Detection uses the accelerometer and device state only — walking is confirmed from a cadence between 1.2–2.5 Hz held for eight seconds. App control is by category, never by app name, and Phone & Texts can never be blocked — a child must always be able to call for help.

What JoanX will not do

Three refusals, and what each one costs us

It never locks the phone

The strongest thing it can do is say something firmer. We give up the hard stop, and keep the app a child will leave installed.

There is no chat

Children visit each other's rooms, leave a like, and write one screened line in a guestbook. That is the entire social layer, on purpose.

You do not watch them live

No map, no location trail, no live feed. A calm weekly summary, and an alert only when something actually happened.

Walk through it yourself
